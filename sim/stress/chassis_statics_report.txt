Why the studies are not solved (SolidWorks 2026 SP2.1 Student, Simulation API, 2026-11-17)
  The chassis is a weldment, so Simulation meshes the tubes as beams and joins them at joints. Beam loads and fixtures go on joints.
  CWJoints (BeamManager.GetJointGroup) can recalculate joints but has no getter: the API hands out no joint objects.
  Tried at one node (swh ChassisStatics probe), AddForce2 with each selection type: the node's sketch point (taken only as
  face/edge/vertex/point, error 18 as beam joint, 19 as beam), the nearest tube vertex (error 11 for all three, 11 mm off the node anyway),
  the tube body (taken as a whole-beam load or as a point load, error 18 as joint). AddRestraint (fixed) refuses all three (1, 16, 1).
  The forces taken on sketch points are kept in the study, but the solver reports no load: they never reach the joints.
  SelectByID2("", "SIMULATION", node) to pick a joint the way the UI does hung SolidWorks for 30 min (killed and restarted).
  Torsion was changed to a balanced pair of couples with inertia relief so that it needs no fixture; it also failed only on the loads.
  What would work: a person picks the joints in each study (a minute per study; the forces and their values are listed above),
  or a solid-mesh model of the frame with forces on tube faces (not attempted: 0.9 to 1.6 mm walls need a very fine mesh).
